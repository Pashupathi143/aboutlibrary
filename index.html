About Our Library

Our Library

The Library is an integral part of the academic community, supporting teaching, learning, research and innovation. It provides access to a wide range of print and digital information resources to meet the academic and information needs of students, faculty members, researchers and staff.

Our library provides a welcoming and technology-enabled learning environment where users can discover, access and utilize reliable information resources. The library continuously works towards improving its collections, services and facilities in accordance with the changing requirements of the academic community.

The library collection includes textbooks, reference books, journals, newspapers, e-books, e-journals, databases, institutional resources and other scholarly materials. Through its services and facilities, the library encourages independent learning, research and lifelong learning.

Library Vision

To develop a modern, user-focused and innovative library that provides equitable access to quality information resources and supports academic excellence, research, knowledge creation and lifelong learning.

Library Mission

To provide effective information resources, services and learning spaces that support teaching, learning and research. The library is committed to delivering user-oriented services, promoting information literacy and enabling users to access reliable scholarly information through print and digital resources.
